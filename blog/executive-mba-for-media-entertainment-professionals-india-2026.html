Why Media & Entertainment Professionals Are Choosing Executive MBA

India's media and entertainment industry is undergoing structural transformation — OTT platforms (Netflix, Prime, Hotstar, JioCinema, Sony LIV), digital advertising, gaming, and creator economy are all expanding at 15-25% annually. This growth creates demand for business leaders who understand both content and commercial management. Media professionals with executive MBA credentials are accessing CEO, COO, and Business Head roles that pure creative or editorial backgrounds cannot reach.

MICA vs IIM for Media Professionals

MICA (Mudra Institute of Communications) Ahmedabad is India's only management school specifically focused on communications, media, and creative management. For media professionals staying in the creative industry, MICA PGDM-C is more directly relevant than a general IIM programme. For media professionals pivoting to general business or digital platforms, IIM executive credentials carry broader recognition.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MBA useful for media professionals in India?

Yes — specifically for media professionals targeting business leadership (CEO, COO, Business Head) at OTT platforms, digital media companies, advertising agencies, and production houses. MBA adds P&L management, financial modelling, strategy, and team scaling frameworks that content expertise alone lacks.

Which MBA is best for journalism and media professionals?

MICA Ahmedabad PGDM-C for media-specific management. IIM Ahmedabad AGMP for strongest general management brand. IIM Nagpur Blended MBA for full degree without leaving current role. ISB PGP for premium brand and VC network if pivoting to media ventures.

Can a journalist do IIM executive program?

Yes — IIM executive programs have no functional restriction. Journalists, editors, and media professionals with minimum experience (3-10 years depending on program) are eligible. IIM programs consider professional experience holistically — media leadership experience is valued in cohort selection.
