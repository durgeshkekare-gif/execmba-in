August 24, 2026 · 7 min read · execmba.in

The ROI Question — What Does an IIM Programme Actually Return?

IIM executive programmes range from Rs 1.75L (IIM Kozhikode Analytics) to Rs 12.10L (IIM Nagpur Blended MBA). The ROI question is the same for all of them: does the career change enabled by the credential pay back the investment, and in how long? The answer depends on three variables: your starting salary, the salary change the credential produces, and how actively you use the credential.

ROI Framework — The Three Variables

For all programmes, break-even within 2 years requires a salary increase of roughly 50% of the total cost — which at a starting salary of Rs 15L, means a 25-50% salary increase. Batch 1 data for IIM Nagpur suggests this range is achievable for professionals who actively change jobs within 18 months of completion.

What Kills ROI — The Most Common Mistake

The single biggest ROI killer: completing an IIM programme and changing nothing about your career approach. The credential is a tool — it requires active use. Professionals who read Batch 1 outcomes first understand this pattern and plan their post-completion career moves in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ROI of IIM executive programmes?

ROI of IIM executive programmes depends on programme cost, salary increase achieved and speed of use. Most programmes break even within 2-3 years when the professional actively changes roles or employers within 18 months of completion. IIM Nagpur Batch 1 success stories provide the first real documented ROI data for the Blended MBA specifically.

Is IIM Nagpur MBA worth Rs 12 lakh?

For working professionals with 3-10 years experience targeting senior management roles: yes, based on Batch 1 documented outcomes. The Rs 14.28L all-in cost breaks even in approximately 2-3 years when a 30-50% salary increase is achieved through active credential use. Read Batch 1 success stories for the most honest assessment of actual outcomes.

Which IIM executive programme has the best ROI?

Shorter, lower-cost IIM programmes (IIM Kozhikode Analytics at ~Rs 2.5L, IIM Nagpur Data Science at ~Rs 2.1L) have the fastest break-even. IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (Rs 14.28L) has the highest absolute return potential because it produces the largest career impact — but requires longer break-even period. ROI choice depends on budget, time horizon and career target role.
