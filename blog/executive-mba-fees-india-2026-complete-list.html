Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest executive MBA from IIM in India 2026?

The most affordable IIM executive programmes are: IIM Mumbai Project Management (₹1.65L, 6 months) and IIM Mumbai Corporate Finance (₹1.75L, 6 months). For senior management credentials: IIM Trichy SMP (₹3.60L, 1 year) is the most affordable PG Certificate from a government IIM for 10+ year professionals.

What is the fee for IIM executive MBA 2026?

IIM executive programme fees 2026: IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (degree) ₹12.10L · IIM Ahmedabad AGMP ₹8.00L · IIM Kozhikode NAMP ₹4.00L · IIM Trichy SMP ₹3.60L · IIM Trichy eGMP ₹2.5L · IIM Kozhikode Analytics ₹2.1L · IIM Ahmedabad FRCG ₹1.85L · IIM Indore AI+Cyber ₹1.95L · IIM Mumbai Finance ₹1.75L · IIM Mumbai PM ₹1.65L

Which executive MBA gives best ROI in India 2026?

ROI depends on career stage. For general management / C-suite track: IIM Ahmedabad AGMP (₹8L, India's #1 IIM brand). For senior professionals (10+ yrs): IIM Kozhikode NAMP (₹4L, specifically designed for this band). For MBA degree: IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (₹12.10L, only IIM MBA degree). For finance track: IIM Ahmedabad FRCG (₹1.85L, IIMA brand at low fee).
