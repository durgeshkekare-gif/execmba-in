IIM Kozhikode Analytics & GenAI — What It Is

The Professional Certificate Programme in Analytics & Generative AI by IIM Kozhikode is a 6-8 month executive programme covering GenAI for business, analytics strategy, and data-driven decision making. ~₹2.1L + GST. Online with IIM-K campus. IIM Kozhikode is NIRF #3 Management, EQUIS + AMBA accredited. Gives IIM Kozhikode alumni status.

What You Actually Learn

  • Generative AI for business: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot for business productivity
  • Analytics strategy: how to build data-driven decision processes in an organisation
  • Business intelligence: dashboards, KPI frameworks, data interpretation
  • Predictive analytics: understanding ML concepts without building models
  • GenAI implementation: evaluating AI tools, building AI use cases, governing AI in organisations
  • Data storytelling: communicating insights to leadership and board

Who Is This For — Honest Assessment

This programme works best for: business managers (not data scientists) who need to lead AI/analytics initiatives; professionals who want IIM-K credentials alongside AI upskilling; and senior managers preparing for roles where AI literacy is now expected.

It is NOT the right programme if: you want to build ML models or write code; you already have strong technical analytics skills and need advanced ML; you are a data scientist wanting IIT-level technical depth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Kozhikode Analytics & GenAI worth it?

Yes for business managers and senior professionals who want IIM credentials combined with practical AI/GenAI literacy. At ~₹2.1L for 6-8 months, it's one of the most cost-effective IIM credentials available with direct career relevance in 2026. The NIRF #3 / EQUIS+AMBA accreditation makes IIM-K alumni status genuinely valuable.

What is the difference between IIM Kozhikode NAMP and Analytics programme?

NAMP (New Age Management Programme, ₹4L, 10 months) requires 10+ years of experience and focuses on strategic leadership for senior professionals. Analytics & GenAI (~₹2.1L, 6-8 months) requires only 2 years experience and focuses specifically on AI/analytics capability. Different target profiles, different purposes.

Does IIM Kozhikode Analytics programme give alumni status?

Yes — completing the programme gives IIM Kozhikode executive alumni status, which is lifetime and includes access to the IIM-K alumni network, events and institutional association.
