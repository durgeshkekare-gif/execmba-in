IIM Ahmedabad AGMP vs IIM Kozhikode NAMP 2026 — Which Is Right for You?

August 27, 2026 · 7 min read · execmba.in

AGMP vs NAMP — Two of India's Best Senior Management Programmes

IIM Ahmedabad AGMP and IIM Kozhikode NAMP are frequently compared by senior professionals because they overlap in purpose — both are for experienced working professionals, both give IIM alumni status, both are online with campus modules. The critical differences are IIM brand strength, fee, eligibility requirement, and accreditation profile.

The Brand Difference — Does IIMA vs IIM-K Matter?

IIM Ahmedabad has India's single strongest management institutional brand — #1 NIRF for 6 consecutive years, Triple Crown (only 1% of business schools globally hold this). The IIM-A credential carries higher recognition in senior hiring across all industries. IIM Kozhikode (#3) is excellent and significantly stronger than most IIMs, but the IIMA premium is real and measurable in shortlisting at Director/VP level.

When to Choose AGMP

  • You have 5-9 years experience — NAMP requires 10+ (firm requirement)
  • IIM Ahmedabad brand specifically is your objective
  • You can budget Rs 9.44L — the IIM-A premium justifies the higher investment
  • You want Triple Crown accreditation (AACSB+AMBA+EQUIS) for global career moves

When to Choose NAMP

  • You have 10+ years experience and meet the strict NAMP requirement
  • Rs 4.72L vs Rs 9.44L — cost efficiency is a priority
  • IIM Kozhikode (#3) brand is sufficient for your career target
  • You are based in South India where IIM-K alumni network is denser

The Third Option — IIM Nagpur Blended MBA

Both AGMP and NAMP give certificates. If you specifically need an MBA degree — for a job description requirement, internal promotion criterion, or international use — IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (Rs 12.10L, 2 years) is the only IIM that gives a full UGC-DEB MBA degree for working professionals. Frequently Asked Questions

IIM Ahmedabad AGMP or IIM Kozhikode NAMP — which is better?

For maximum IIM brand impact: AGMP (IIM-A is #1). For best value at 10+ years experience: NAMP (IIM-K #3 at Rs 4.72L vs Rs 9.44L for AGMP). Both give IIM alumni status. AGMP requires only 5 years experience; NAMP requires 10 years minimum. Choose based on your experience level, budget and whether IIM-A brand specifically is important.

Is IIM Kozhikode NAMP worth Rs 4 lakh?

Yes — IIM Kozhikode is NIRF #3 with EQUIS+AMBA dual accreditation. At Rs 4.72L all-in for 10 months, NAMP is excellent value for 10+ year professionals targeting C-suite. Compared to IIM-A AGMP at Rs 9.44L, it delivers comparable management learning and IIM alumni status at about half the cost.

Can I do AGMP with 5 years experience?

Yes — IIM Ahmedabad AGMP requires minimum 5 years post-qualification work experience. NAMP requires 10 years. If you have 5-9 years experience, AGMP is the right choice between the two. If you have under 5 years, consider IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (3 years minimum) or IIM short-term certificates.
