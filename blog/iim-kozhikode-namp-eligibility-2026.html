IIM Kozhikode NAMP — Eligibility in Brief

NAMP Eligibility: Graduate from any recognised university + minimum 10 years of post-qualification work experience. No CAT/GMAT required. Selection based on work experience quality and a selection process by IIM Kozhikode.

Who Is the Ideal NAMP Candidate?

NAMP was designed for a specific profile: a senior professional who has 10+ years of deep operational expertise in their function and is now targeting VP, Director, COO, or CXO roles. The curriculum does not teach management basics — it starts from the assumption that you already know operations, finance, HR, or marketing deeply, and builds strategic leadership frameworks on top.

  • Director / VP / Senior VP level professionals
  • Professionals with 10-18 years in one industry transitioning to general management
  • C-suite aspirants who need management credibility to match their operational depth
  • Entrepreneurs with 10+ years who want structured management frameworks
  • Senior PSU officers targeting executive management roles

How IIM Kozhikode Selects NAMP Candidates

NAMP selection is application-based. IIM Kozhikode evaluates: quality of work experience (progression, responsibility, impact), current seniority level, alignment with programme objectives (C-suite aspiration), and application/statement of purpose. There is typically a short-listing discussion or interview with IIM-K faculty. The cohort is curated for experience diversity — professionals from diverse industries and functions are preferred over homogeneous cohorts.

Application Fee and Process

  • Application fee: ₹2,000 + GST (non-refundable)
  • Apply via Jaro Education partner portal
  • Documents: degree certificate, experience letters (all employers for 10 yrs), ID proof, photograph
  • Programme fee: ₹4,00,000 + GST (on selection and offer acceptance)
  • Instalment structure: typically 3 instalments over 10 months

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum experience for IIM Kozhikode NAMP?

IIM Kozhikode NAMP requires a firm minimum of 10 years of post-qualification work experience. This is not a guideline — candidates with fewer than 10 years are not eligible. The programme is specifically designed for senior professionals, and the curriculum assumes this level of operational experience.

Is GMAT or CAT required for IIM Kozhikode NAMP?

No — NAMP does not require CAT or GMAT scores. Selection is based on work experience quality, professional seniority, and the IIM Kozhikode selection process (application + potential interview). This makes NAMP accessible to senior professionals who are not test-takers.

Is 10 years experience the same as 10 years in one company?

No — the 10-year requirement counts total post-qualification work experience across all employers. If you worked 4 years at Company A, 3 years at Company B, and 3 years at Company C — total 10 years — you meet the criteria. What matters is the aggregate experience and its quality, not tenure at a single company.
